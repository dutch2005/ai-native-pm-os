# Module 10.1 — Choose Your PM OS Capstone

**Time:** 5 minutes (to read and choose)  
**Outcome:** Select the capstone project that will cement your PM OS workflow.

---

## The Final Step

You have built the infrastructure. You have the vault, the strategy docs, the discovery pipelines, the MCP connectors, and the prototyping loops.

Now, you must prove the system works on a complete, end-to-end project.

Choose one of the three Capstone tracks below. You will use this track for the remainder of Module 10.

---

## Option A: The "Zero to One" Track (New Product)

**The Scenario:** Meridian is launching a completely new product line: "Meridian Insights" — an AI-powered analytics add-on that predicts workflow bottlenecks before they happen.

**Choose this if:**
- You are a PM focused on new product development, 0→1 initiatives, or startup environments.
- You want to practice TAM/SAM/SOM, strategic narrative, and initial feature scoping.

**Your Capstone Deliverables will be:**
1. A Strategic One-Pager (Why build this?)
2. A Lightweight Spec for the MVP feature
3. A landing page prototype

---

## Option B: The "Scale & Optimize" Track (Growth/Core PM)

**The Scenario:** Meridian's core "Approval Routing" feature has high adoption, but activation is slipping. Users set up workflows but abandon them before running them.

**Choose this if:**
- You are a PM focused on core product, growth, funnel optimization, or enterprise scaling.
- You want to practice data analysis, A/B test design, and incremental improvement specs.

**Your Capstone Deliverables will be:**
1. A Narrative Analytics memo identifying the drop-off
2. An A/B Test Design Brief to fix it
3. An interactive prototype of the proposed onboarding fix

---

## Option C: The "Technical Infrastructure" Track (Platform PM)

**The Scenario:** Meridian's API usage has skyrocketed. You need to build a developer portal and implement rate limiting to protect the core infrastructure, while keeping enterprise customers happy.

**Choose this if:**
- You are a Platform PM, Technical PM, or handle complex system integrations.
- You want to practice technical PRD writing, edge case mapping, and internal stakeholder alignment.

**Your Capstone Deliverables will be:**
1. A full PRD (Platform level) focusing on edge cases and rate limits
2. A Stakeholder Matrix and Handoff Memo
3. A Jira ticket pipeline generated from the PRD

---

## Make Your Choice

Once you have selected your track, proceed to the corresponding module to begin your capstone.

- If Option A: `/start-m10-2a`
- If Option B: `/start-m10-2b`
- If Option C: `/start-m10-2c`
